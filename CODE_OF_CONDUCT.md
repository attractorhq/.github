# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a friendly, safe, and inclusive community. We welcome
people regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and make room for different perspectives.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, never on people.
- Protect private information and respect stated boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[opensource@attractor.fi](mailto:opensource@attractor.fi). Maintainers will
review reports promptly, preserve confidentiality where possible, and take
proportionate action. Possible actions include a warning, removal of content,
temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public.
